# EA-TTM Horizon Performance Summary

This document summarizes the line-graph evidence created from the locked final-test outputs.

## Key interpretation points

The horizon-wise plots show how error evolves from day 1 to day 28, rather than compressing the full horizon into a single aggregate metric.

The strongest overall MSE gain of the primary EA-TTM over frozen HP-TTM occurs at horizon 18, while the weakest overall MSE gain occurs at horizon 6.

For extreme-only MSE, the strongest gain occurs at horizon 13, while the weakest gain occurs at horizon 6.

These plots should be used in the report and defense to show whether the model's advantage is concentrated in early, middle, or late forecast steps.

The extreme-prevalence line graph should be shown alongside the error curves so that the reader can interpret strong or weak horizon-specific gains in the context of how many extreme observations are actually present at each step.
